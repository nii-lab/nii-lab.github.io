Motor equations:
  • Va=−KaKtωV_a=-K_aK_t\omega
  • i=Va−KbωRai=\frac{V_a-K_b\omega}{R_a}
  • Jdωdt+bω=Kmi+TdJ\frac{d\omega}{dt}+b\omega=K_mi+T_d
  1. Substitute the expression for VaV_a into the expression for current i. Then substitute the resulting expression for i into the equation of motion, and rearrange as a differential equation relating ω and TdT_d.
  2. Set the initial conditions to 0 and apply the Laplace transform to this differential equation. Collect terms containing Ω(s)\Omega(s) on one side, then find Ω(s)Td(s)\frac{\Omega(s)}{T_d(s)}.

Find: The minimum amplifier gain satisfying the tolerance condition.

  • Steady-state tolerance: |ωss|≤0.5|\omega_{ss}|\le0.5 rad/s.

  1. Substitute Td=DT_d=D, dωdt=0\frac{d\omega}{dt}=0, and ω=ωss\omega=\omega_{ss} into the differential equation derived above. Divide both sides by the coefficient of ωss\omega_{ss} to express ωss\omega_{ss} as a function of KaK_a.
